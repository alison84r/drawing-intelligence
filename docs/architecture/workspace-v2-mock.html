Three decisions

1 · Left panel: full work list, or keep today's queue and add an "All balloons" group under it?My recommendation: full work list. One list, problems on top.
2 · Table headers: hide the icons until hover plus one Filter button, or remove column filters completely?My recommendation: hide until hover. Power users keep the filters; nobody sees them by default.
3 · Filter chips: keep the strip across the top, or move it into the left panel?My recommendation: move it. The strip costs a full row of height and the list is what it filters.

Timing: the demo is tomorrow. The table clean-up (headers, one Filter button, footer, indent sub-rows) is the small, safe part. The work list and the never-empty right panel are larger and safer after the demo.
