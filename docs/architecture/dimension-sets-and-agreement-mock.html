Finding dimensions as complete sets, and trusting them only when two finders agree

Two changes to the recognizer core. Both come from the published work on dimension recognition, need no training data, and aim at the 95% target and at an honest confidence.

What limits us
  • A dimension is found from its two arrowheads only
  • One callout printed on two lines becomes several balloons
  • Confidence is 80% or 100%, nothing in between
After items 1 and 2
  • A dimension is a full set of parts that belong together
  • Each balloon says how many independent checks found it
  • A scored answer key proves the percentage
Item 1

A dimension is a set: text, dimension line, arrowheads, extension lines

Today we use the two arrowheads and the line between them. The drafting standards define five parts. Using all five tells us which feature is measured and lets one arrowhead, or none, still be understood.

What it adds

  • Chain and baseline dimensions, where several values share one extension line
  • Arrow styles we miss today: ticks, dots, open arrows. The extension lines still mark the ends
  • Which feature each dimension measures, so a hole callout and its depth and thread lines become one balloon instead of four
  • Fewer "line without a value" entries: a set with a missing part is reported as that, with the part named
Item 2

Two finders that work differently, and a confidence that means something

Finder A is what we have: start from arrowheads, look for the text. Finder B is new: start from every value-like text, look for the nearest line that could be its dimension line or leader. They fail in different ways, so agreement between them is real evidence.

Risks

  • This changes the recognizer core. Until the answer key exists we cannot see regressions on the new drawings
  • Extension lines are thin lines, like centre lines and hatching. Telling them apart needs care on dense sheets
  • Recognition will take longer per sheet. Today it is 2 to 15 seconds

What does not change

  • The length check, the views, the tables and title-block protection
  • Saved inspections: existing balloons keep their place and status
  • The fallback: tag v0.5.1-demo is today's tested build
