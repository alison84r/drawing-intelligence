What changes, in plain words

  • Left panel becomes a work queue. Three groups that open and close: what needs a decision, what could be added, facts about the sheet.
  • Every problem says what is wrong. Balloon number, the value, and one sentence. Click it and the sheet zooms there with the balloon pulsing.
  • Right panel shows the callout itself. A small picture of what was read, then the requirement, then the checks as a short list with ticks and crosses.
  • One status vocabulary everywhere. To check, Flagged, To measure, Pass, Fail. Same pill in the list, the grid, the panel and on the sheet.
  • Cues. Keyboard hints on the buttons, tooltips on every icon, a toast after each action, a "what next" line when a group is empty.
  • The sheet fills the space. In your screenshot it sits at 18% in a large empty area; fit should use the room.

Review and Measure: do we need both?

They are two moments of the same job, not two screens. My proposal removes the tabs and keeps one list.

My pickA · One workspace, statuses instead of tabs The top bar only shows progress (Read → Check → Measure → Report). The chips under it choose what you see. A balloon moves To check → To measure → Pass or Fail. The result box unlocks when the balloon is accepted. This is how Jira and Monday treat work: one board, items change status.
B · Keep two steps, rename and explain them "1 Check balloons" and "2 Enter results", each with a one-line hint and a "done, go to results" button when the first is complete. Smaller change, but the list still appears twice with different columns.
