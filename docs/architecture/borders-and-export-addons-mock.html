  • Desk behind the sheet: a soft grey-blue background, with the white drawing lying on it under a light shadow. The sheet edge becomes obvious.
  • Panels as cards: queue, table and detail each get a rounded border, a light shadow and a small gap between them.
  • The panel you are working in is marked: a navy line on top of the detail panel when a balloon is selected.
  • Table: tinted header band, and the selected row carries a navy bar at its left edge, the same blue as its balloon on the drawing.
  • Colour stays quiet: navy for "active", red only for fail, as today.

2 · Export add-ons

The site you sent is a CNC and moulding supplier. Their quality page lists what such a supplier hands to a customer with the parts. That is a good checklist for what our export could carry beyond the forms.

The export has today

AS9102 Forms 1 · 2 · 3PPAP dimensional resultsBallooned PDF with stamp and legend
QuickCertificate of ConformanceOne signed page: part, revision, lot, quantity, "meets drawing", result summary. Every shipment needs one.
QuickInspection planA "Method" column per balloon (micrometer, pin gauge, thread gauge, CMM), guessed from the feature type, editable. Exported as the plan the inspector walks through.
QuickCMM exchange (CSV)Balloon list out with nominal and limits; measured values back in from the CMM file. Removes typing of results.
QuickTraceability headerMaterial, heat / lot, sample number, and a disposition per part: accept · hold · rework. Printed on every form.
MediumRelease package (ZIP)Ballooned PDF, forms, CoC and attached material certificates in one file, with a manifest and checksums for audit.
MediumNonconformance reportFor every failed balloon: requirement, measured value, deviation, crop of the drawing, disposition and sign-off.
MediumCustomer template mappingFill the customer's own FAI spreadsheet instead of ours, by mapping our columns to theirs once.
LargerCapability (Cp / Cpk)Several samples per balloon, then a capability sheet for key characteristics. Needs multi-sample result entry first.
LargerTolerance risk summaryMarks each balloon routine · review · high-risk from how tight it is for its size. Useful for quoting, before any part is made.

My order: Certificate of Conformance and traceability header first (small, every customer asks), then inspection plan and CMM exchange (saves the most typing), then the release package.
