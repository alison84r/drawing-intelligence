Sub-balloons on a hole callout: today and two cleaner layouts

Your screenshot shows the problem: four balloons in a row, four leaders that cross each other, and 24.3 sitting on the next dimension.

Today

A row with crossing leaders

  • Leaders cross; hard to tell which is which.
  • The row runs into balloon 25 and its dimension.
Option A · recommended

One balloon, one leader, a chain

  • One leader from 24 to the callout. Sub-balloons touch it as a chain: .1 .2 .3.
  • Click .3 and its text (↧12) lights up; no permanent leader needed.
  • Shortest footprint, so it stays clear of neighbours. The exported PDF looks the same.
Option B

Each balloon beside its own row

  • Row 1 gets 24 and .1, row 2 gets .2 and .3, right of the text.
  • Reads like the callout, but needs free space to the right, which is often where the next dimension sits.

Both options also make the placement treat the whole group as one block, so it no longer lands on a neighbouring balloon or dimension.
