Choices I made on the open questions

stepsThe steps are places, not gatesYou can click Measure before Review is finished. The counts tell you what is left; nothing locks.
measuringGrid for measuring, panel for reviewingIn Measure the grid grows and gains Min, Max and an editable Result. In Review the right panel leads.
left panelIt stays, and it collapsesThe rail keeps it one click away. "Focus on the drawing" hides both panels for a clean sheet.
dark modeLighter blue, same paperNavy is too dark on a dark screen, so the accent lightens. The drawing stays white, slightly dimmed.

How I would build it

1Colour and typeNavy primary, one type scale, themed grid. Low risk, changes every screen at once.
2Top bar and stepsBreadcrumb, step bar with live counts, primary Export.
3Canvas tools and settings drawerFloating bars, rail, drawer. Left panel reduced to three blocks.
4Inspector and grid modesRequirement-first panel, "and next" flow, Review and Measure column sets.
