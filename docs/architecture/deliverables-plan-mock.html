The new point in your screenshot: CTQ balloons

  • Suggested automatically: fits (H7, g6), geometric tolerances, tolerances tighter than the general table, and anything the drawing marks with a flag or key symbol.
  • You confirm: one click sets or clears CTQ; nothing is CTQ without your say.
  • Shown everywhere: a distinct balloon on the drawing, a CTQ chip in the table and work list, a filter "CTQ only", and a CTQ count in the stamp and legend.
  • Report: CTQ rows marked in Form 3 (designator column) and a one-page CTQ list.

The picture uses red only so the idea is easy to see. In the app, red stays reserved for "fail"; CTQ would get its own shape (for example a diamond or a double ring) in navy.

From the brake-disc case study: control plan and CTQ validation record

The case study and the PPAP paragraph you sent name two more records. Both grow out of the CTQ flag: once a balloon is CTQ, it needs a "why", a "how it is checked" and a result.

#CTQ featureWhy it mattersMethodHow oftenIf it failsResult

  • Control plan = the first six columns, for every CTQ balloon. Method is suggested from the feature type; "why", frequency and reaction are typed once and reused on the next revision.
  • CTQ validation record = the same rows with the measured result, method and sign-off. It is the CTQ slice of Form 3.
  • These are the PPAP elements the supplier lists: control plan, traceability, CTQ validation.

Order I would build it in

1 · CTQ on the ballooned drawing

Suggest, confirm, show, export. Completes document 1.

2 · Certificate of Conformance + traceability

Lot, heat, sample serial, disposition; one CoC page; attach material certs. Completes documents 3 and 5.

3 · Control plan + CMM report

"Method", frequency and reaction per CTQ; balloon list out as CSV, measured values back in; CTQ validation record. Completes document 2 and the PPAP elements.

4 · Release package

All five documents in one ZIP with a checksum manifest.
